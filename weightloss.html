QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

How does weight loss treatment work?

You complete a health assessment, then a licensed clinician reviews your information and recommends a plan when clinically appropriate.

How quickly will I see results?

Results vary by person. Your clinician will help you set realistic goals and track your progress over time.

What happens after I place my order?

Your care team remains available for support and follow-up as your treatment progresses.

Can I stop or change my treatment?

Talk with your clinician before changing treatment. Your plan can evolve as your health and goals change.
